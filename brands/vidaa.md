# Hisense, Toshiba and others running VIDAA

VIDAA is Hisense's own platform. Its privacy menu is less consistent between regions than the
larger vendors, and some models additionally carry a third-party ACR vendor.

## Turn off

`Settings` → `System` → `Privacy` (sometimes `Settings` → `Support` → `Privacy` or
`Settings` → `System` → `Legal & Privacy`):

- **Viewing Information / Viewing History** → Off
- **Interest-Based Advertising** / **Personalized Ads** → Off
- **Usage & Diagnostics** / **Device Data** → Off
- **Voice Services** → Off unless used
- **Terms of Use / Privacy Agreements** → decline everything optional

Also look for a separately branded ACR item — **Samba TV**, **Alphonso**, or a vendor-named
"Interactive TV" — under the same menu or during setup.

## Known behaviour

- Menu locations vary substantially by region and firmware on VIDAA. If your set does not match the
  above, [please open an issue with your model and what you see](../CONTRIBUTING.md) — this page has
  the thinnest coverage in the repo and contributions genuinely improve it.
- Some VIDAA models are quite persistent about re-prompting for consent after updates.
