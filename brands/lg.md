# LG (webOS)

**The setting you are looking for is called "Live Plus."** That is LG's name for ACR.

## Turn off ACR

- **webOS 6 and newer:** `Settings` → `All Settings` → `General` → `System` → `Additional Settings`
  → **Live Plus** → Off
- **webOS 4–5:** `Settings` → `All Settings` → `General` → **Live Plus** → Off
- **Older webOS:** `Settings` → `All Settings` → `General` → `Additional Settings` → **Live Plus**

## Also turn off

`Settings` → `All Settings` → `General` → `System` → `Additional Settings` (or under
`Support` → `Privacy & Terms` on some versions):

- **Advertisement** → **Limit Ad Tracking** → On (this one is On = good; it is the odd one out)
- **Home Promotion** / **Home Settings** → promotional content → Off
- **User Agreements** / **Privacy & Terms** → decline everything except the mandatory privacy policy.
  Look specifically for **Voice Information**, **Viewing Information**, and **Interest-Based
  Advertising**.
- **Voice Recognition** → off unless used

## Reset the advertising ID

Under **Advertisement**, there is usually a **Reset Advertising ID** option. Use it after opting out.

## Known behaviour

- LG's user agreements screen presents several toggles that look mandatory. Only the top-level
  privacy policy and terms of use are required for the TV to function. The rest are optional even
  when the layout suggests otherwise.
- Major webOS updates have historically re-presented the agreements screen. Re-check after updates.
- Turning off Live Plus does not disable the LG Content Store or the ad row on the home screen.
  Network blocking handles those.
