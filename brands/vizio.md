# VIZIO (SmartCast)

**The setting you are looking for is called "Viewing Data."** VIZIO's ACR system is called
**Inscape**.

This is the company the FTC sued in 2017 for doing exactly this without consent, on about 11 million
televisions, including sets sold before the feature existed that had it added by firmware update.
See [../evidence/ftc-vizio-2017.md](../evidence/ftc-vizio-2017.md).

## Turn off ACR

- **SmartCast TVs:** `Menu` → `System` → `Reset & Admin` → **Viewing Data** → Off
- **Older SmartCast:** `Menu` → `System` → `Reset & Admin` → **Smart Interactivity** → Off
- **Pre-SmartCast:** `Menu` → `System` → `Reset & Admin` → **Smart Interactivity** → Off

The feature has been called **Smart Interactivity**, **Viewing Data**, and **Inscape** across
generations. Turn off whichever you find.

## Also turn off

- `System` → `Reset & Admin` → **Advertising** → **Limit Ad Tracking** → On
- **Reset Advertising ID** — do it after opting out
- During setup, decline **Viewing Data** at the prompt rather than accepting and turning it off later

## Why VIZIO is the clearest case

VIZIO's own SEC filings, made when it went public in 2021, disclose that its Platform+ segment —
advertising and viewing data — produced **more gross profit than the entire hardware business**,
on a fraction of the revenue. The television is the customer acquisition cost. See
[../evidence/vizio-sec-filings.md](../evidence/vizio-sec-filings.md).

This is not unique to VIZIO. It is simply the best-documented instance, because they had to tell
investors the truth in writing.
